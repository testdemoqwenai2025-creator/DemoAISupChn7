1. VIDEO OVERVIEW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Title: "Don't Just See Your Supply Chain. Command It."
Duration: 3-4 minutes
Format: 16:9 (1920x1080) + 9:16 vertical cut for social media
Target Audience: Investors, enterprise clients, seed funds, startup accelerators
Tone: Professional, innovative, authoritative, visually stunning
Style: Screen recording + motion graphics + data visualization animation

Key Message: This platform transforms supply chain management from reactive
to proactive using AI, real-time data, and automation.


2. CREATIVE DIRECTION & DESIGN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

COLOR PALETTE:
  Primary:    #0a0e1a (deep navy background)
  Accent 1:   #10B981 (emerald green — operational/AI)
  Accent 2:   #06B6D4 (cyan — data/real-time)
  Accent 3:   #8b5cf6 (purple — intelligence/forecasting)
  Accent 4:   #ef4444 (red — alerts/critical)
  Text:       #e2e8f0 (light gray)
  Highlight:  #f59e0b (amber — warnings/value)

TYPOGRAPHY:
  Headlines:  Inter Bold (800 weight)
  Body:       Inter Regular (400 weight)
  Data:       Monaco / JetBrains Mono (monospace for numbers)
  Callouts:   Inter SemiBold (600 weight, uppercase, letter-spaced)

VISUAL MOTIFS:
  - Pulsing dots (representing real-time data)
  - Flowing lines (representing supply chain connections)
  - Animated bar charts (data in motion)
  - Glowing borders (active monitoring)
  - Radar sweep effect (scanning/monitoring)

MOTION PRINCIPLES:
  - Smooth transitions (300ms ease-out)
  - Data counters animate up from 0
  - Charts draw in from left to right
  - Panels slide in from edges
  - Pulsing effects on "live" indicators

5. VISUAL EFFECTS & TRANSITIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TRANSITIONS BETWEEN SCENES:
  - Scene 1→2: Zoom into globe → dissolve to Command Center
  - Scene 2→3: Slide left to reveal Ports module
  - Scene 3→4: Cross-dissolve with green "AI" flash
  - Scene 4→5: Screen split effect (digital twin scenario)
  - Scene 5→6: Number counter transition ($6.8M → $1.7M)
  - Scene 6→7: Quick cut with keyboard shortcut visual
  - Scene 7→8: Pull back / zoom out to globe

DATA ANIMATIONS:
  - Counters: animate from 0 to final value over 1.5 seconds
  - Charts: draw in from left to right (SVG stroke-dashoffset animation)
  - Donut charts: conic-gradient sweep from 0 to final percentage
  - Bar charts: grow from bottom to height over 0.8 seconds
  - Health gauge: fill from 0 to score with color transition

HIGHLIGHT EFFECTS:
  - Red pulse on critical alerts (box-shadow animation)
  - Green glow on AI savings (radial gradient pulse)
  - Blue ripple on real-time data updates
  - Purple shimmer on AI/ML predictions


6. MUSIC & SOUND DESIGN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

MUSIC STYLE: Cinematic orchestral with electronic elements
  - Deep bass pulses (matching the LIVE indicator)
  - Building strings for tension scenes (port congestion)
  - Triumphant brass for savings/ROI reveals
  - Minimalist piano for narration-heavy sections

SOUND EFFECTS:
  - Soft click on UI interactions
  - Data ping on new alert
  - Cash register chime on financial savings reveal
  - Whoosh on scene transitions
  - Low rumble on critical alerts

TEMPO:
  - 0:00-0:15: Building (60 → 90 BPM)
  - 0:15-1:40: Steady (90 BPM — operational pace)
  - 1:40-2:15: Dramatic (120 BPM — tension during scenario)
  - 2:15-3:05: Triumphant (100 BPM — resolution)
  - 3:05-3:30: Finale (120 → 60 BPM — slow resolution)


7. PRODUCTION NOTES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

RECORDING:
  - Use 1920x1080 screen recording at 60fps
  - Record in dark mode (better for video contrast)
  - Pre-load all data before recording (avoid loading spinners)
  - Use Chrome with DevTools closed
  - Disable notifications during recording

POST-PRODUCTION:
  - Add motion graphics in After Effects or similar
  - Color grade: enhance contrast, boost accent colors
  - Add text overlays with consistent positioning
  - Export at 1080p H.264 for web, 4K for presentations
  - Create 9:16 vertical cut for LinkedIn/social media

ALTERNATIVE CUTS:
  - 30-second teaser (Scenes 1, 5, 8 only — the wow moments)
  - 60-second investor pitch (Scenes 1, 2, 5, 6, 8)
  - 3-minute full demo (all scenes)
  - 5-minute deep dive (add extended narration for each module)
