## Key Capabilities

- **Real-Time Risk Monitoring** — Continuous scanning of global supply chain nodes with AI-driven risk scoring and alerting across 12+ risk categories including geopolitical, climate, financial, and regulatory risks.
- **Predictive Analytics** — Machine learning models forecast disruptions 30-90 days in advance with 94%+ accuracy, enabling proactive mitigation strategies.
- **Regulatory Compliance** — Automated tracking and reporting for GDPR, SOX, ISO 28000, C-TPAT, AEO, and 40+ regulatory frameworks across 60+ jurisdictions.
- **Multi-Industry Coverage** — Purpose-built intelligence for financial services, manufacturing, agriculture, energy, pharmaceuticals, technology, retail, and government sectors.
- **News Intelligence** — Aggregated feeds from AP Press, Yahoo Finance, Wall Street Journal, and global finance sources covering agriculture to high-frequency trading.
- **Command Center** — Interactive operational dashboard for real-time decision-making with map visualisations, alert management, and team coordination.
