Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with the platform?
You can start with a free 14-day trial that includes full access to all features. Our onboarding team will help you set up your supplier data and configure initial risk models. No credit card required to start.
What integrations are available?
We offer pre-built connectors for SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Workday, Kinaxis, Blue Yonder, Coupa, and 50+ other enterprise platforms. Our REST API also enables custom integrations with any system.
How does the AI explain its predictions?
Every prediction includes SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values showing exactly which factors contributed to the risk score and by how much. This full transparency builds trust and enables better decision-making.
What compliance frameworks do you support?
We provide dedicated tracking for UFLPA (Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act), EUDR (European Deforestation Regulation), CSDDD (Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive), GDPR, SOX, REACH, and can customize for additional frameworks upon request.
How is my data protected?
We're SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and use AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit. Data is hosted in your choice of AWS, Azure, or GCP regions. We also offer on-premise deployment options for enterprise customers.
